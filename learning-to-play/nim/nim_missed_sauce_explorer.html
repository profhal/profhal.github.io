Nim: Understanding Missed Sauce Opportunities

Explore the identity m(n) = c(n) × o(n): missed opportunities per game = concession fraction × opportunities per game. Eight agents against NaiveRandom, plus an NR-versus-NR baseline; 100,000 games per pile size and player order. Hover or tap for details.

Definitions
c(n) = missed opportunities / sauce opportunities (decimal, 0–1)
o(n) = sauce opportunities / games
m(n) = missed opportunities / games = c(n) · o(n)
g(n) = [m(n+4) − m(n)] / 4 = c(n+4)·[o(n+4)−o(n)]/4 + o(n)·[c(n+4)−c(n)]/4 = g_o(n) + g_c(n)
go(n) = c(n+4)·[o(n+4)−o(n)]/4
gc(n) = o(n)·[c(n+4)−c(n)]/4

Sources: eight-agent versus-Random tournament and separate Random-versus-Random experiment (NR baseline), not the four-agent round robin. NC1 is Constant1 in the source. All fractions are decimals, not percentages. The 4-stick difference compares piles n and n+4; the two contribution curves sum exactly to g(n) whenever both concession fractions are defined. Negative values indicate decreases. Missing c(n) means no sauce opportunities occurred; for NR playing first at n=4, c(4) is undefined, not zero.
